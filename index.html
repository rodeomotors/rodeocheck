What RodeoCheck can and cannot prove

Public NHTSA data can decode the vehicle and surface safety information. Complete title, accident, insurance-loss, service, ownership and commercial auction-history datasets are not universally free. RodeoCheck keeps those gaps explicit instead of treating “no result” as a clean history.

IPHONE APP

Add RodeoCheck to your Home Screen

  1. Host this folder on an HTTPS site such as GitHub Pages.
  2. Open the RodeoCheck URL in Safari on your iPhone.
  3. Tap the Share button.
  4. Choose Add to Home Screen.
  5. Tap Add. RodeoCheck will launch like a standalone app.

On iPhone, the normal web “Install” prompt may not appear; Safari → Share → Add to Home Screen is the reliable method.

Full-history sources remain separate

For title-brand/odometer history, use an approved NMVTIS provider. For free participating-insurer theft/salvage/flood screening, use NICB VINCheck. RodeoCheck links to these services rather than scraping, bypassing limits or pretending to have proprietary data.
